Opening questions and follow-up branches

1. Would this exact kit get a first-time user to the written kiosk task with no further hardware?

If yesCan we tick each included supply, storage, cable, cooling and enclosure item and identify anything assumed to be already owned?
If noWhich omitted item blocks the task, and where is that disclosed in the listing?
If vaguePlease compare the task and the itemised listing rather than calling the kit complete; what would I still need on the desk?
Public evidencePublic kit contents, compatibility notes and a reviewed unpurchased parts checklist.
PurposeThe practical unit of use is a functioning system, while company board metrics count something narrower.
Your queue

2. Is your published commercial-service process available to a genuine small deployment without a custom hardware design?

If yesWhat inputs, image ownership, test acceptance and minimum requirements should an actual buyer prepare?
If noWhich service or partner normally handles that need instead?
If vagueDescribe a hypothetical educational ten-device setup as a process example only; I am not asking for a quote or implying an order.
Public evidenceExisting service description, generic checklist or publicly shareable blank acceptance form.
PurposeThis locates the labour between a retail board and a repeatable deployment.
Your queue

3. When a customer reports no boot, is there a documented path to separate board, supply, storage image and cable problems?

If yesWhich evidence should the user gather before support can route it, and who owns the next step?
If noWhich published support route is the correct starting point and what should a user avoid replacing prematurely?
If vagueUse my current real setup log, or a clearly hypothetical example if no fault exists; do not create a return for this discussion.
Public evidencePublished troubleshooting/returns policy and a minimal nonconfidential problem-report template.
PurposeSupport work may sit with the reseller even where the technical platform is common.
Your queue

What to ask to see—and what this person cannot establish

Evidence requests

  • Exact kit contents and accessory models from public product pages.
  • A blank commissioning/acceptance checklist if publicly shareable.
  • Policy names and support-routing responsibilities, dated at the observation.

Limits of this contact

  • A reseller cannot speak for Pi’s total accessory margin or customer mix.
  • A commercial-services page is not evidence of the volume of such services.
  • The Delaware shipment address does not establish visitor or showroom access.
