A defined eight-SKU availability panel

How do public availability and delivered-cost fields vary across channels for identical products?

Define the test before you start

Operator task: At the same chosen local time twice each week for four weeks, read the normal public product page for each fixed product/seller pair without reserving stock.

Success rule: Eight dated snapshots with exact variant matching or a reason-coded missing cell. A complete record includes unknowns; no stock-derived sales figure is produced.

Materials

  • A manual spreadsheet with eight fixed rows and four chosen US seller columns: DigiKey, Newark, PiShop and Micro Center St. Davids.
  • Freeze exact manufacturer numbers for: one Pi 5 RAM variant, one Pi 4 variant, one CM5 storage/wireless/RAM variant, one CM4 variant, one Pico 2 variant, the Pi 5 supply, one supported storage card and one case/cooler. Resolve numbers from official listings before the first observation.

60–90 minute setup; 20–30 minutes per snapshot; 30 minutes definitions review. No orders, subscriptions or automation performed.

Controls, stopping rules and limits

Hold these conditions fixed

  • Never substitute a different RAM, wireless, eMMC, packaging or bundled variant to fill a gap.
  • Keep shipping/tax separately stated when a comparable delivered total is unavailable.
  • Stock movement is not a measure of sell-through, customer demand or inventory owned.

Stop when

  • Respect normal access and rate limits; do not bypass blocked pages.
  • If a product is removed, retain the missing row and annotate any replacement as a new series.

What this cannot establish

  • Four websites are a convenience panel.
  • No displayed quantity may be inferred from “in stock”; no amount sold may be inferred from a decline.
