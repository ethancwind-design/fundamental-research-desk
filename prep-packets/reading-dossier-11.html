Compare alternatives for one job, not one specification

Prepare a comparison the analyst can perform for a specified workload. The reading set covers a Raspberry Pi board/module, an industrial module and a Jetson production-platform boundary; it does not rank them or claim to cover the whole market.

What you should be able to explain

  • Hand the comparison protocol to another engineer and have them reproduce the configurations. Explain which requirements each source establishes and which can only be resolved by testing or a customer decision; leave the final ranking to your own work.

Where the remaining evidence comes from

Public sources

  • Official specifications, revision ledgers, lifecycle policies and software support documents.

Your terminal

  • Use licensed industry research to expand the candidate universe and retrieve independent benchmark methodology; do not equate a market-share table with suitability for this job.

Your conversations and observations

  • Ask an engineer who has shipped products on at least two platforms to critique the workload and test boundaries.
  • Run the same workload on borrowed or lab hardware if available; record setup and maintenance effort as well as performance.
  • Ask purchasing for a hypothetical comparable-volume quote through a normal disclosed inquiry if you later choose to contact vendors.

Evidence to have before moving on

  • A one-page workload specification with pass/fail requirements.
  • A full-system bill-of-components and support matrix for each candidate.
  • A user-run test protocol with software version, load, temperature, power and repeatability recorded.
