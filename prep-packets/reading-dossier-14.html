Carbon claims: boundary, model and physical change

Build a methodology ledger for sustainability statements. Distinguish measured activity, modeled embodied emissions, operational use, purchased credits and changes to the calculation itself.

What you should be able to explain

  • Explain one carbon claim with its boundary, data, model and time basis. State whether the reported change came from a physical process, activity level or revised calculation, and identify the evidence still needed to reproduce it.

Where the remaining evidence comes from

Public sources

  • Annual SECR/TCFD methodology, prospectus comparison, later silicon-model article and named research-tool links.

Your terminal

  • Use licensed ESG data only with its source year, scope and restatement treatment; obtain underlying methodology rather than treating a vendor score as physical evidence.

Your conversations and observations

  • Ask the sustainability lead or named LCA partner for the publicly shareable calculation boundary and treatment of model changes.
  • Ask an engineer to review an equal-workload power-measurement protocol and observe the test.
  • Ask a manufacturing practitioner which process variables would substantiate the claimed change, using public examples.

Evidence to have before moving on

  • A claim/method/boundary/source ledger.
  • A model-change chronology and restatement questions.
  • A user-run equal-workload energy protocol; an explicit separation of emissions and credits.
