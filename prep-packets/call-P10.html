1. For my written local-display task, are all of these required items included in this exact kit?

If yesCan we match the included supply, cable, storage, cooling and case to the printed list?
If noWhich additional product is necessary, and is the missing item obvious on the shelf label?
If vaguePlease explain one concrete compatibility question; I do not need a claim that the kit is popular.

What to ask to see—and what this person cannot establish

Evidence requests

  • Six-row shelf/web/kit checklist with timestamps and exact model variants.
  • Three actual points of ambiguity and the public answer, if obtained.
  • A reason-coded missing-data field: unavailable, not found, label unclear, staff unavailable or not observed.

Limits of this contact

  • Store staff cannot disclose or represent the company’s total demand or end-market mix.
  • Shelf facings, a queue and one employee’s impressions are not sell-through data.
  • Do not interview or record shoppers without their voluntary agreement.
