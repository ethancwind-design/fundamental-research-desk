What to ask to see—and what this person cannot establish

Evidence requests

  • A source-linked dictionary of direct units, licensee units, microcontroller units, MSRP-weighted ASP, royalty revenue and component revenue.
  • Existing public APM reconciliation and the source of any historical definition change.
  • A public replay/transcript route or confirmation that a full transcript is not publicly provided.

Limits of this contact

  • IR cannot provide an independent view of end-user experience or represent all customers.
  • This call cannot turn undisclosed channel sell-through, current trading or a new forecast into public facts.
  • An unrecorded oral explanation is not a replacement for an amended filing; retain attribution and request the public basis.
