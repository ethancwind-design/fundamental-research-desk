Engineering work, development assets and amortization

Learn how engineering effort becomes an expense, a capitalized development asset, an amortization charge or a cash outflow. Keep the product-development narrative separate from accounting judgments.

What you should be able to explain

  • Explain one pound or dollar of engineering effort through cash flow, expense and asset recognition using a hypothetical example that follows the published policy. Then reconcile the report’s actual totals without double counting non-cash additions.

Definitions to keep separate

Development capitalization
The report sets out criteria for recognizing qualifying development expenditure as an intangible asset; research and maintenance do not automatically qualify.Physical pp103–104 · p103 ↗ (physical PDF page 103)
Useful economic life
An accounting estimate of the period over which an asset is amortized; it is distinct from a product’s promised manufacturing lifetime.Physical pp104, 111 · p104 ↗ (physical PDF page 104)
Non-cash capital addition
The capital expenditure reconciliation includes costs reallocated into development assets that are not a new cash payment in that period.Physical p32 · p32 ↗ (physical PDF page 32)
