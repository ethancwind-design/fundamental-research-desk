Six matched project-choice interviews

Which real constraints appear in current, lapsed and alternative-platform project histories?

Define the test before you start

Operator task: Conduct the same core 15-minute project interview, capturing a real decision date, task, candidate platforms and source of the stated decisive constraint.

Success rule: Each completed row has a first-hand role, concrete task and evidence type. Missing sample categories remain visibly unfilled.

Materials

  • One common interview guide from P09; anonymous respondent IDs; voluntary attribution choices.
  • A six-slot recruitment sheet: current Pi 2, former Pi 2, alternative chosen 2.

4–6 active hours over about two weeks if volunteers respond; no payments or messages sent.

Repeatable procedure

Step 1

DoRecruit through at least two pools and cap any single referral chain at two participants. Ask eligibility before sentiment.
RecordApproaches/eligible/declined counts by pool; no unnecessary names.

Step 2

DoUse the same P09 opening sequence; ask the project task before asking why Pi was chosen or rejected.
RecordTask, role, stage, date, alternatives, criterion.

Step 3

DoRequest one optional corroborating artefact and the distinction between measured-at-the-time versus recollected explanation.
RecordPublic link or no artefact; chronology evidence.

Step 4

DoRecord a fixed set of context tags after the interview: prototype/deployed/abandoned, skill level, software requirements, I/O, availability, total-system cost, support, and other. Keep the participant’s wording.
RecordComparable context plus original wording.

Step 5

DoSend only that participant’s factual chronology for optional correction if they agreed. Preserve corrections and conflicts.
RecordOriginal/corrected fields; permission.

Step 6

DoClose with a six-row evidence table and recruitment gaps. Do not calculate customer churn or market share from the sample.
RecordCompleted/unfilled slots; contradiction list; remaining recruitment bias.

Controls, stopping rules and limits

Hold these conditions fixed

  • A former user must have actually used Pi; a general critic does not fill the slot.
  • An abandoned project is coded separately from a platform replacement.
  • Do not discard a weakly corroborated story; retain it with the weaker evidence label.

Stop when

  • One polite invitation and, where appropriate, one reminder; accept refusal.
  • No paid or employer-confidential project details assumed.

What this cannot establish

  • Purposeful six-person sample is qualitative.
  • Recruitment success and balanced coverage are not guaranteed; report unfilled slots.
