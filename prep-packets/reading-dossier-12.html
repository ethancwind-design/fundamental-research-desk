Where the remaining evidence comes from

Public sources

  • Prospectus market definitions and footnotes; annual route-to-market and revenue note; regional product approvals.

Your terminal

  • Retrieve the cited VDC/other industry reports if entitled, preserving edition and market definition. Refresh geographic and end-market research separately from company-reported accounting geography.

Your conversations and observations

  • Ask IR how it derives the outside-UK narrative percentage and whether a public reconciliation exists.
  • Ask a regional reseller which approvals and support tasks it actually performs for a completed OEM project.
  • Ask a customer where its design and procurement decisions occur; do not infer those locations from its headquarters.

Evidence to have before moving on

  • A market-claim dictionary with years and denominators.
  • An addressable-market input tree without a new market-size estimate.
  • A customer-versus-end-use geography reconciliation and unresolved-definition list.
