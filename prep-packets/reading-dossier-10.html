Definitions to keep separate

Named application evidence
A named OEM product can establish that a technology is used in that application; it does not necessarily disclose units, contract duration or supplier revenue.Physical p90 · p90 ↗ (physical PDF page 90)

Where the remaining evidence comes from

Public sources

  • OEM manuals/datasheets, company case studies and public product announcements; these do not generally reveal supplier-specific revenue.

Your terminal

  • Find historical research interviews and customer references; preserve anonymization, sample size and whether the source independently verified adoption.

Your conversations and observations

  • Ask product engineering, purchasing and field-service roles about the same completed design; compare their accounts.
  • Seek integrators who use several module vendors, and explicitly ask for examples where Raspberry Pi was not selected.
  • Ask permission to observe a finished product’s setup or maintenance; document what you actually see.

Evidence to have before moving on

  • A customer evidence ledger with exact product, source, stage and scale boundary.
  • At least one OEM-origin document for each application studied.
  • A sample-selection log and separate queues for adopted, rejected, stopped and replaced designs.
