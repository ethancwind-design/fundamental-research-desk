Components, contract manufacturers and committed cash

Trace board and semiconductor supply separately, then connect the physical lead times to inventory ownership and purchase commitments.

What you should be able to explain

  • Draw the path from a memory purchase commitment to an assembled board and then cash collection. Explain which balances appear before shipment, after shipment and only in the commitments note, using the company’s actual accounting language.
